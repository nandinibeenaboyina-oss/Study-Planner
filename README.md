# Student Study Planner & Exam Preparation System

A full-featured study planning, exam preparation, syllabus tracking, and campus placement web application for B.Tech CSE students.

---

👉 **[Open Live Study Planner App](https://study-planner-4-yt8j.onrender.com/)**

2. Render will automatically redeploy the new build (or click **Manual Deploy > Deploy latest commit** in your Render dashboard).

## ✨ What's New in this Update

### 1. 📚 Syllabus for Each Subject with Direct Resource Links
- **Topic-by-Topic Syllabus:** Detailed syllabus units for all subjects (DBMS, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Data Structures & Algorithms, Machine Learning, Aptitude, etc.).
- **Direct YouTube Video Link (`YouTube Video` button):** One-tap button opening the exact curated YouTube lecture (Gate Smashers, Abdul Bari, Striver, StatQuest, etc.) in a new tab.
- **Direct Google Notes Link (`Google Notes` button):** One-tap button opening the matching Google search / GeeksforGeeks / PDF notes for that specific topic.
- **Completion Checkbox:** Mark topics as completed to dynamically update your syllabus percentage bar.
- **Add Custom Topics:** Add your own syllabus units, lecture videos, and notes URLs.

### 2. ⏱️ Focus Study Timer (Pomodoro & Stopwatch)
- **Modes:** 25-minute Pomodoro, 50-minute Deep Work, 45-minute Power Hour, and an Open Stopwatch.
- **Subject-Specific Tracking:** Pick which subject you are studying while the timer runs.
- **Automatic Logging:** Once completed, sessions automatically log to your study hours analytics and progress reports!
- **Glowing Visual Ring:** Circular countdown timer with pause, play, and reset controls.

### 3. 🌌 Premium Obsidian Black & Radiant Violet Theme
- **Color Palette:** Deep obsidian black (`#090714`), radiant violet (`#8b5cf6`, `#7c3aed`, `#6d28d9`), and glowing borders (`#2a1f4a`).
- **Glassmorphic Glow:** Subtle ambient violet radial gradient background (`violet-glow-bg`) with high-contrast readable typography.
